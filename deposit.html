Deposit with CptMarkets

If you would like to deposit funds into your CptMarkets account, please contact our online customer service first. Our support team will guide you through the available deposit process and provide the appropriate instructions for your request.

For a smooth and secure deposit experience, it is recommended that you follow the instructions and guidance provided by the official customer service team.

Before You Deposit

Please contact online customer service

Deposit instructions can depend on the current payment method and account requirements. Please speak with customer service before making a payment so that you can receive the correct instructions for your account.
